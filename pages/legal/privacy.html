Privacy Policy

Web2App Studio Pro is designed primarily as a browser-based application builder. Project settings and saved app history may be stored locally in your browser.

Information handling

Do not enter confidential credentials, private keys, payment information, or other sensitive information into project fields unless a dedicated secure service explicitly requires it.

Third-party services

The application may load third-party libraries and fonts from external CDNs. Their own privacy policies and technical practices apply to requests made to those services.

Your control

You can clear locally stored project information through your browser storage controls or the application's project-management controls.
