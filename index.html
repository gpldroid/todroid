Documentation & Frequently Asked Questions

How do I install the generated APK file on my Android device?

After clicking Download APK File, open the downloaded `.apk` file on your phone. If prompted, enable "Allow installation from unknown sources" in your Android Security Settings.

Can I publish this application to Google Play Store?

Yes! Use the Download Source (.ZIP) option, open the project in Android Studio, sign it with your production Keystore, and generate an Android App Bundle (.aab) for Google Play Console.
